How Sunwai Works

End-to-end voice loop using sovereign Indian AI

1

1. Spoken Student Reasoning

The student solves the question out loud in English, Hindi, or Marathi, explaining why they arrived at their answer.

2

2. Sarvam AI Diagnostics

Sarvam Saaras transcribes the speech, and Sarvam 105B diagnoses the conceptual flaw against our 6-skill taxonomy.

3

3. Bulbul Voice Reteaching

Sarvam Bulbul speaks a simple, real-life story (rupees, rotis, sweets) and asks a follow-up question to lock in understanding.
